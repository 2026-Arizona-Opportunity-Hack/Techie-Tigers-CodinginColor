# User Guide: Coding in Color Donor Platform

This guide explains how to sign in, move around the website, and do common tasks. It is
written for Coding in Color staff and board members. You don't need any technical
knowledge to use it.

## 1. Signing in and out

**To sign in:**

1. Open the website. You will land on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your work email and password. Click the eye icon to show or hide your password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

The website knows your role and takes you straight to your own workspace.

**To sign out:** click **Sign out** at the bottom of the left-hand menu.

> If you don't have an account yet, ask the CEO / admin to create one for you.

## 2. Getting around the website

Every page has the same layout:

- **Left-hand menu (sidebar):** the list of pages you can open. The page you're on is
  highlighted. At the top it shows your workspace name, for example
  "Board Member Workspace".
- **Main area:** the page you picked.

**On a phone or small screen,** the menu is hidden. Tap the menu icon (☰) at the top
left to open it, and tap a page name or the **X** to close it.

**Keyboard users:** every button and link works with the keyboard. Press **Tab** to
move forward, **Shift + Tab** to move back, and **Enter** to choose.

**Getting lost:** if you open an old or wrong link, the website takes you back to your
Overview page.

## 4. Pages for everyone

### Overview / My Dashboard

The first page you see after signing in. It gives you a quick summary.

- **CEO / Admin and Fundraising:** money raised this year, the retention rate, new and
  returning donors, how much was lost to lapsed donors, and a table showing which board
  members have been reaching out to donors (donors contacted, last outreach, overdue
  follow-ups).
- **Board members:** your own week: overdue follow-ups, what's due this week, drafts
  waiting for review, and donors you haven't contacted in 90 days.

Shortcut buttons on this page take you straight to the pages you'll need next, such as
Outreach, Follow-ups or Import donors.

### Donors (All Donors / My Donors)

A list of donors with their type, owner (board member), total given, status and next
step.

- **Search:** type a name or email in the search box.
- **Filter:** use the drop-downs to show only **Individuals** or **Funders**, only
  donors owned by one board member, or only donors with no gift this year or last.
- **Open a donor:** click **Open** on any row to see their full page.
- **Next step:** you can add a short note of what to do next (for example "Invite to
  Day of Compute") and tick it off when it's done.

### A donor's page

Click **Open** on any donor to see:

- **Contact Information:** email and phone, with buttons to email, text or call them.
- **Totals:** total given, number of gifts, and when they were last thanked.
- **Retention badge:** whether they gave more, less or the same as last year.
- **Timeline:** every gift, message and conversation, newest first. Next to a gift, click
  **Thank** to create a thank-you draft. A gift that's already been thanked shows
  **✓ Thanked**.
- **Draft update with AI** or **Draft quarterly update:** creates a personal update for
  this donor. The draft goes to the Outreach page for you to check before sending.
- **Funder Report** (funders only, for CEO / Admin and Fundraising): click
  **Open report** for a printable summary of the funder's giving and the outcomes it
  paid for, or **Gift history (CSV)** to download a spreadsheet of every gift.
- **Owner** (CEO / Admin only): choose which board member is in charge of this donor.

### Retention

Shows which donors kept giving from one year to the next.

1. Pick a year using the year buttons at the top.
2. See the retention rate and a chart of donors by status (each status has a text
   label, not just a color, and the chart also has a table view).
3. The **call list** shows lapsed donors, biggest givers first, so you know who to
   contact. For the current year it's titled "Call before December 31".

See [Retention labels explained](#8-retention-labels-explained) below.

## 5. Pages for board members

### Follow-ups

Your to-do list for donors, in three groups:

- **Overdue:** follow-ups that are past their date.
- **Due this week:** follow-ups coming up soon.
- **Owed a quarterly touch:** donors who haven't heard from you this quarter. Click
  **Draft update** to start a message.

You can also add your own follow-up (for example "Invite to Day of Compute") and click
**Save follow-up**.

### Outreach

Where you review and send messages. **The website never sends anything by itself.** A
person always checks and sends every message.

- **Drafts to review:** thank-yous and updates waiting for you.
- **Sent history:** messages that have already gone out.
- **Log a conversation:** record a call, text or in-person chat so it shows on the
  donor's timeline.

See [Send a thank-you or update](#send-a-thank-you-or-update) for step-by-step
instructions.

## 6. Pages for the CEO / admin

### CSV Imports

Add donors and gifts from a file. You always see a preview before anything is saved.
See [Import a donor file](#import-a-donor-file) below.

### Duplicate Review

Sometimes the same person appears twice (for example once from Zeffy and once from the
spreadsheet). This page shows possible pairs side by side. Fields that differ are in
**bold**.

- Pick the record to keep with **Keep this one**, then click **Merge into …**. Blank
  contact details on the kept record are filled in from the other one.
- If they are really two different people, click **Not a duplicate**.

### Board Members

A list of board members with how many donors they have and how much they've raised.
Search by name, and click **Open** to see one member's donors, totals and retention.
From a member's page you can also **Mark inactive** or **Reactivate** them.
(Fundraising staff can see this page too.)

### Programs

Add Coding in Color's programs (for example AI Academy, Summer Camp, Day of Compute) and
their yearly outcomes (for example "45 students across 3 schools; 82% completion").
These outcomes are used in donor updates and funder reports, so donors hear what their
gift did.

- Type a name and click **Add program**.
- Under a program, add an outcome with a year and description, then click
  **Add outcome**.
- You can rename or delete programs and outcomes.

### API Keys

Keys that let Coding in Color's own dashboard read donation totals. They never share
donor names or contact details.

1. Type a name (for example "CIC org dashboard") and click **Create key**.
2. Click **Copy** right away. **The key is only shown once.**
3. To stop a key working, click **Revoke**. This takes effect immediately.

For setup details, see [read-api.md](read-api.md).

## 7. Common tasks, step by step

### Find a donor

1. Click **All Donors** (or **My Donors**) in the left-hand menu.
2. Type their name or email in the search box.
3. Click **Open** on their row.

### Send a thank-you or update

1. Go to **Outreach** in the left-hand menu.
2. Under **Drafts to review**, click a message to open it.
3. Read it and make any changes.
4. Choose how to send it: email or text message.
5. Click **Open in Gmail** or **Open in Messages**. Your own email or messaging app opens
   with the message filled in.
6. Press **Send** in Gmail or Messages.
7. Come back to the website and click **Mark as sent**. Only do this after you've
   actually sent it.

If you don't want to send a draft, click **Dismiss draft**. It won't be sent.

### Log a phone call or meeting

1. Go to **Outreach** and click **Log a conversation**.
2. Pick the donor and how you talked (for example phone or in person).
3. Write a short note (for example "Coffee chat. Not ready to give yet, wants to see a
   class.").
4. Click **Save to timeline**.

### Import a donor file (CEO / admin)

1. Click **CSV Imports** in the left-hand menu.
2. **Step 1:** choose where the file is from:
   - **CIC spreadsheet:** the team's own donor sheet
   - **Zeffy:** online donations export
   - **Benevity:** workplace giving report
3. **Step 2:** choose the file (.csv or .xlsx, up to 5 MB).
4. Click **Preview import**. Nothing is saved yet. You'll see how many rows were read,
   new donors, gifts, possible duplicates, and any rows that will be skipped and why.
5. If it looks right, click **Confirm import**. Otherwise click **Start over**.
6. Possible duplicates go to **Duplicate Review** for you to check.

Sample files to practise with are in [sample-data](sample-data/).

### Send a funder their report (CEO / admin and Fundraising)

1. Open the funder's page from **All Donors** (filter by **Funders** to find them faster).
2. In the **Funder Report** box, click **Open report**.
3. Print it, or save it as a PDF from your browser's print menu, and send it to the
   funder. Or click **Gift history (CSV)** for a spreadsheet.

> If the report doesn't open, your browser may have blocked the new tab. Allow pop-ups
> for this site and try again.

### Give a donor to a board member (CEO / admin)

1. Open the donor's page.
2. Use the owner picker to choose a board member.

Tip: the Overview page shows donors without an owner. Click **Assign them** to find
them.

## 8. Retention labels explained

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | First gift this year |
| **Gave more** | Gave more than last year |
| **Gave the same** | Gave the same as last year |
| **Gave less** | Gave less than last year |
| **Came back** | Gave before, skipped last year, and is back this year |
| **Lapsed** | Gave last year, but not this year |
| **Not yet this year** | Gave last year and hasn't given yet this year (the year isn't over) |

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please enter your email and password." | Fill in both boxes before clicking **Sign in**. |
| "No active account for this login" | Your account isn't set up or was turned off. Ask the CEO / admin. |
| "Invalid or expired token" | Sign out and sign in again. If it keeps happening, contact the tech team. |
| "Can't reach the server." | The website can't connect right now. Wait a minute and refresh the page. If it keeps happening, contact the tech team. |
| "Too many requests. Try again in N seconds." | You've used AI drafting or imports a lot in a short time. Wait the number of seconds shown and try again. |
| "AI drafting isn't available right now…" | The AI helper is down, so a standard template was used instead. You can still edit and send it. |
| "Your browser blocked the new tab." | Allow pop-ups for this site in your browser settings and try again. |
| I can't see a page someone mentioned | That page is for a different role. See [What you see depends on your role](#3-what-you-see-depends-on-your-role). |
| I can only see some donors | Board members only see the donors assigned to them. Ask the CEO / admin if a donor should be yours. |

Still stuck? Contact the Coding in Color CEO / admin.
